Fried rice chicken slice recipe

Fried rice is often a go-to choice for breakfast or packed meals because it is simple to prepare. The ingredients and seasonings can be adapted based on what is available at home.

Ingredients

  • 150g Chicken slice
  • 2 Eggs
  • 5 shallots
  • 5 clovet of garlic
  • 2 churly red chilies

Instructions

  1. Heat Rose Brand cooking oil. Sauté the shallots and garlic until fragrant, then set aside.
  2. Add the eggs and scramble until cooked.
  3. Add the chili and shredded chicken, and mix well.
  4. Masukkan nasi, garam dan micin Asatu Rose Brand atau kaldu bubuk, aduk rata. Koreksi rasa. Sajikan dengan irisan timun, tomat dan kerupuk. Enak banget rasanya
